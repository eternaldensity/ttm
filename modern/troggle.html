How to play (after the 1994 original)

Story: Robot-TROG (run by Dr. FrankenTroggle) grabbed the Muncher. Scientist Magenta sends you — her troggle-sniffing dog Sparky — through 8 levels to get clues and save the day.

Loop per level: open 3 chests → each needs a grade-level story problem. One chest = clue (advance), two = treats.

Troggles: when Sparky howls, hit Troggulate and enter an equation equal to the troggle count (e.g. 8 → 4+4). Wrong/slow = troggles munch a whole treat box. Lose all 3 → Sparky goes home.

Energy: troggulating costs 10, phone costs 5. Chests give +10. Run a 60-second drill to recharge.

Grades 1–7: 1–2 add/subtract small; 3–4 add/sub/mult/div bigger; 5–7 fractions, decimals, multi-step. The original tags problems like [#123]; this tribute filters the same way.
